## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & FEASIBILITY VERDICT

### 1.1 Study Purpose

This feasibility study evaluates the growth viability of **Ayola Foods Kenya** — an existing Nairobi-based health food restaurant and packaged food producer founded by food scientist Prisca Kiragu (JKUAT). The study assesses the feasibility of scaling operations from the current single-location model to a multi-channel business with retail distribution, food delivery platform presence, and nationwide packaged product distribution.

### 1.2 Overall Feasibility Verdict

| Dimension | Rating | Score (1–10) |
|---|---|---|
| Market Feasibility | **HIGHLY FEASIBLE** | 9.0/10 |
| Technical Feasibility | **HIGHLY FEASIBLE** | 8.5/10 |
| Financial Feasibility | **FEASIBLE** | 7.5/10 |
| Operational Feasibility | **FEASIBLE** | 7.5/10 |
| Legal & Regulatory | **HIGHLY FEASIBLE** | 8.0/10 |
| Organizational Feasibility | **FEASIBLE** | 7.0/10 |
| **OVERALL VERDICT** | **HIGHLY FEASIBLE — SCALE WITH CONFIDENCE** | **7.9/10** |

### 1.3 Key Conclusions

1. **The business is already proven** — Ayola Foods has paying customers, repeat orders, a growing Facebook following (~1,471 likes), and active social media engagement. This is not a concept — it's a validated business.
2. **The market timing is excellent** — gut health, probiotics, and functional foods are the #1 global food trend, and Kenya is 2–3 years behind. Ayola has first-mover advantage.
3. **The founder is uniquely qualified** — Prisca Kiragu's food science training (JKUAT) provides genuine technical credibility that competitors cannot easily replicate.
4. **Scaling is the primary challenge** — the business needs investment in KEBS certification, packaging, delivery infrastructure, and team to move from a single restaurant to a multi-channel brand.
5. **The product-market fit is strong** — unique products (plantain kvass, synbiotic porridge, unconventional combos) differentiate Ayola from all competitors in the Nairobi market.

### 1.4 Recommendation

**SCALE the business with investment of KES 3.3–6.8M (Phase 1)**, prioritizing:
1. KEBS certification for packaged products
2. Professional packaging for retail readiness
3. Listing on Glovo/Bolt Food delivery platforms
4. Hiring a kitchen manager to free the founder for growth strategy
