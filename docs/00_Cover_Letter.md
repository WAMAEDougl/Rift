### About Ayola Foods Kenya

Ayola Foods is a health food restaurant and packaged products business in Kahawa Sukari, Nairobi. Founded by food scientist Prisca Kiragu (JKUAT), it combines ancestral Kenyan ingredients with modern food science to create probiotic beverages, heritage flour blends, and nutritious ready meals.

**Products:** Plantain probiotic kvass, synbiotic porridge, goat milk tea, heritage ugali and uji flour blends, ready meal combos featuring unconventional proteins (rabbit, turkey eggs).

**What makes Ayola different:** Every product is formulated by a trained food scientist — not marketing, but real nutrition science. Kenya's first plantain-based probiotic beverage. Affordable health food for everyday Kenyans, not just the wealthy.
