## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### The Opportunity

Kenya's food market is projected to reach **USD 79.3 billion by 2029** (Statista), yet the health and functional food segment remains drastically underserved — dominated by expensive imports inaccessible to most Kenyans. With **58 million people** (75% under 35), rising rates of lifestyle diseases, and exploding interest in gut health and probiotics, there is massive unmet demand for **affordable, locally produced, science-backed health foods**.

### The Company

**Ayola Foods Kenya** is a Nairobi-based food business founded by **Prisca Kiragu**, a JKUAT-trained food scientist, combining a **full-service health-focused restaurant** with **packaged food products** shipped countrywide. Operating from Ruhan Plaza in Kahawa Sukari (Thika Road corridor), Ayola Foods specializes in:

- **Probiotic and fermented beverages** (plantain kvass, synbiotic porridge)
- **Nutritious unconventional meals** (rabbit wet fry, turkey egg combos, health-focused pilau)
- **Heritage flour blends** (special ugali blend, special uji blend)
- **Vegetarian, vegan, and gut-health-focused options**

### What Makes Ayola Unique

No other food business in Kenya combines all of these under one brand:
1. A food scientist founder with genuine expertise in functional food formulation
2. Probiotic beverages (plantain kvass) — first of its kind in Kenya's food service sector
3. Both ready meals (restaurant) AND packaged products (retail/e-commerce)
4. Heritage African ingredients with modern nutritional science
5. Affordable pricing for middle-class Kenyans
