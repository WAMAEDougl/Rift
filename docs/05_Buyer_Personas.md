## Your Immediate Action Plan

Based on everything in this document, here are the five highest-impact moves you can make in the next 30–90 days:

### 1. Launch a Student Special (Week 1–2)
Create a KES 200 combo (uji drink + light snack). Print simple flyers. Distribute at KU and JKUAT gates. This alone could add KES 50,000–100,000/month from the 110,000 students within walking distance.

### 2. List on Glovo (Week 2–4)
Apply to Glovo Kenya. You'll reach professionals across Nairobi who want your food but can't get to Kahawa Sukari during lunch. Glovo handles delivery — you handle cooking. Peak ordering is lunch (12–2 PM) and Sundays (2–3 PM).

### 3. Create a Family Pack (Week 2–4)
Bundle 3x Ugali Blend + 1x Uji Blend at KES 1,250 (normally KES 1,350 — a KES 100 saving). Market it on Facebook and WhatsApp to mothers. This increases average order size and makes reordering easy.

### 4. Formalize Parcel Shipping (Month 2)
Partner with Sendy or G4S for reliable, trackable countrywide delivery. Offer a flat shipping rate. This turns your occasional parcel customers into monthly subscribers.

### 5. Add Full Nutrition Labels to Packaged Products (Month 2–3)
Work with a nutritionist to get proper per-serving macros (calories, protein, carbs, fiber, iron) on your Ugali and Uji Blend packaging. A peer-reviewed study from Kenya confirmed that consumers pay more for flour products when nutrition information is clearly provided.
